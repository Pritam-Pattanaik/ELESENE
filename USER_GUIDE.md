# USER_GUIDE.md — Customer Guide to Brand Investment & Rewards

## 1. What is the ELESENE Brand Investment Program?
The ELESENE Brand Investment Program recognizes your ongoing relationship and commitment to the ELESENE luxury brand. Every purchase and engagement elevates your standing in our brand tier ecosystem.

> *Note: Brand Investment Points measure lifetime contribution and brand recognition. They do not represent equity, financial securities, dividends, or monetary investment.*

---

## 2. Earning Investment Points (IP) & Loyalty Points (LP)
- **Purchases**: Every ₹1 spent earns 1 Investment Point (IP) and 0.1 Loyalty Point (LP).
- **Product Reviews**: Submit verified product reviews to earn **+20 IP**.
- **Referrals**: Invite friends to join ELESENE and earn **+300 IP** upon their first completed purchase.
- **Profile Completion**: Complete your persona and style profile for **+50 IP**.
- **Social Sharing**: Share your favourite looks for **+25 IP**.

---

## 3. Brand Tiers & Exclusive Privileges
1. **Seed** (0 - 2,999 IP): Access to brand updates and seasonal previews.
2. **Bronze** (3,000 - 7,999 IP): Priority customer service and birthday rewards.
3. **Silver** (8,000 - 14,999 IP): Free standard shipping on all orders.
4. **Gold** (15,000 - 29,999 IP): Early access to new collections (24h priority).
5. **Platinum** (30,000 - 59,999 IP): Dedicated personal stylist consultation and invitations to VIP preview events.
6. **Diamond** (60,000+ IP): Bespoke tailoring assistance and concierge service.

---

## 4. Redeeming Loyalty Points (LP)
- Redeem your spendable LP balance in your account dashboard for shipping vouchers, order discount coupons, or complimentary brand gifts.
- Redeeming LP **never** reduces your lifetime Investment Points or tier status.
